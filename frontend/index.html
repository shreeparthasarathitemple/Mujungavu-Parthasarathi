Sri Parthasarathi Temple Mujungavu

Mujungavu, Kumbla, Kasaragod, Kerala

Temple Introduction

Sri Parthasarathi Temple Mujungavu is a historic Krishna temple located in Mujungavu, Kumbla, Kasaragod, Kerala. The temple is dedicated to Lord Parthasarathi, a form of Lord Krishna. The official website provides information about temple timings, daily poojas, festivals, history, announcements, photographs and other temple-related information.

Temple Timings

Morning: 5:30 AM to 12:30 PM

Evening: 5:30 PM to 7:00 PM

Poojas

  • Usha Pooja
  • Ucha Pooja
  • Athazha Pooja

Festivals

Annual Festival (Utsava), Tula Sankramana, Ashtami Rohini, Vishu and other major Hindu festivals are celebrated with grand devotion.
